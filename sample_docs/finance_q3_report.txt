Internal Finance Report — Q3 Financial Summary

Revenue: Total revenue for Q3 reached $4.2 million, representing a 12% increase compared to Q2. Growth was primarily driven by the enterprise sales segment.

Operating Expenses: Operating expenses totaled $2.8 million, a slight increase of 3% quarter over quarter, mainly due to increased hiring in the engineering department.

Profit Margin: Net profit margin for the quarter stood at 18%, up from 15% in the previous quarter.

Budget Forecast: The finance team projects a 10-15% revenue increase in Q4, driven by seasonal demand and two new enterprise contracts expected to close in November.

Cash Reserves: The company's cash reserves at the end of Q3 stood at $6.5 million, providing an 8-month operating runway at current burn rate.
